Meet the Founder

Kawitha Reddy

Founder, Bangalore Music Trust

Kawitha Reddy founded the Trust out of a long-standing commitment to music, education and artistic growth, and a conviction that strong foundations are what turn beginners into confident performers.

Her teaching is rooted in care, disciplined practice and personal mentorship — every student given individual attention at each stage of their work.

Music is more than a skill.

Learning an instrument teaches attention, patience and the habit of returning to something difficult until it gives. Students who stay with it tend to carry that elsewhere.

And it is social work as much as solitary. Playing with other people, and for them, is how confidence and a sense of belonging are built. That is the part a trust can look after.

Founder

Kawitha Reddy

Founder, Bangalore Music Trust

Kawitha Reddy founded Bangalore Music Trust out of a long-standing commitment to music, education and artistic growth. She believes in building strong foundations — the kind that turn beginners into confident performers.

Her teaching philosophy is rooted in care, disciplined training and personal mentorship, so that every student receives individual attention and encouragement at each stage of their journey.

She is also an Internationally Certified Sound Healing Therapist, working with personalised sound therapy sessions designed to support emotional balance, stress relief and inner clarity.

Alongside her work as an educator and mentor, she is pursuing advanced violin training, continuing to refine her own artistry and bring current performance standards into her teaching.
