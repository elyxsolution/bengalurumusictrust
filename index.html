Who We Are

Bangalore Music Trust is envisioned as a platform that brings together learners, educators, artists and supporters of music, across Indian classical and Western traditions, to create meaningful opportunities for musical education and expression.

  • Learners
  • Educators
  • Artists
  • Supporters

What We Teach

Alongside Hindustani classical music, Bangalore Music Trust offers training in Western vocals and Western musical instruments, for children, young people and adults.

  • Western

    Western Vocals

How to Join

A clear next step for every learner, whether you are enquiring for yourself or for your child.

  1. EnquireRegister your interest online, call the Trust or send a message on WhatsApp.
  2. Hear backThe Trust responds with class details: ages, timings, fees and venue.
  3. BeginAgree the next steps with the Trust and begin your musical journey.

Why We Exist

Every generation deserves the opportunity to discover the power of music.

Bangalore Music Trust seeks to create pathways for musical learning, nurture emerging talent and contribute to a culture where music continues to be valued, practiced and shared.
