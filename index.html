How to join

A clear next step for every learner, whether you are enquiring for yourself or for your child.

  1. Send an enquiry through the Contact page, message the Trust on WhatsApp, or call +91 89711 68006.
  2. The Trust replies with class details, including ages, timings, fees and venue.
  3. Choose the class that suits the learner.
  4. Confirm the enrolment and begin learning.

Music teaches listening, discipline, patience, expression and teamwork. But good teaching is not within everyone's reach.

Bangalore Music Trust wants to change that in Bengaluru. Your support puts a learner in front of a teacher, and an instrument in their hands.

Corporate partnerships (CSR)

Companies and institutions can help bring music education to more learners in Bengaluru through their CSR programmes. The Trust welcomes early conversations. Its registration, tax-exemption and CSR eligibility details will be published once confirmed, so your team can verify eligibility before committing support.

Your company's CSR support can sponsor learners, instruments and performances.
