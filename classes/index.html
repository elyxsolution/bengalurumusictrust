Our Classes

Bangalore Music Trust offers training in Hindustani classical music, Western vocals and Western instruments, for children, young people and adults.

Indian & Western Traditions

What We Teach

Three areas of learning, each shaped around listening, practice and the confidence to perform. Ages, timings, fees and venue are shared when you enquire.

  • 01Western

    Western Vocals

    Breath control, pitch, tone and stage confidence, solo or in a group. Learners develop musical expression and the confidence to perform.

    • Breath control, pitch and tone
    • Singing technique and musical expression
    • Confidence to perform, solo or in a group

    Ages, timings, fees and venue are shared on enquiry.

    Enquire
  • 02Western

    Western Instruments

    Hands-on practice, reading music and ensemble playing, with instruction that builds technique and confidence over time.

    • Keyboard, string and percussion instruments
    • Reading music and understanding technique
    • Ensemble playing and performance experience

    Instruments offered, ages, timings, fees and venue are shared on enquiry.

    Enquire
  • 03Indian classical

    Hindustani Classical

    Guided learning in the Hindustani classical tradition, built on close listening, patient practice and respect for a living musical heritage.

    • Swara, tala and the foundations of raga
    • Voice training in the Hindustani tradition
    • Learning through listening, repetition and practice

    Ages, timings, fees and venue are shared on enquiry.

    Enquire

Registering an interest does not confirm a place in any class. The Trust shares class details with every enquiry.
