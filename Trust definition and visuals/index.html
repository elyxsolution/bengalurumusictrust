About the evening

Every term at the Trust ends the same way: the students who have been working in our rooms since June play for whoever wants to come. The programme is chosen by the students with their teachers, and runs for about ninety minutes with one interval.

Doors open half an hour before. Children are welcome, and there is no dress code.

About the Trust

Music education, performance and access.

Bengaluru Music Trust was founded around the belief that music education, performance and access to music can create meaningful opportunities for people and communities.

The Trust supports music education, nurtures young and emerging musicians, and creates opportunities for learning and performance. We run classes and workshops through the year, present concerts open to the public, and contribute to the wider musical and cultural life of the city.

Everything we do is paid for by fees, donations and grants. Where fees would keep someone out, we cover them.

Venue & directions

Finding us

The music rooms

Two teaching rooms, a small hall that seats a hundred and twenty, and a courtyard where most people end up after a concert. The hall has a tuned upright and a full drum kit; instruments can be borrowed for practice by arrangement.

The building is step-free from the street, and there is an accessible toilet on the ground floor.
